SIDS, Global Aviation Connectivity & Statistical Methodology

Small Island Developing States (SIDS) play a critical role in maintaining global aviation networks, serving as key transit hubs and air routes. This research emphasizes that SIDS are not a monolith—their distinct economic, geographic, and institutional profiles require tailored safety oversight strategies rather than one-size-fits-all policies.

Applied Chi-Square independence testing to civil aviation compliance data to evaluate how state capacity influences Safety Management System (SMS) implementation under ICAO Annex 19 across diverse island jurisdictions.
