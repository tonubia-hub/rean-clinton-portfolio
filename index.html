Governance & Research Case Studies

Quantitative Analysis of Global Aviation Safety Compliance (ICAO Annex 19 & SIDS)
+

Situation

Small Island Developing States (SIDS) face acute resource and technical constraints balancing rigorous ICAO Annex 19 safety mandates against state capacity limitations.

Task & Methodology

Executed Pearson’s Chi-Square Tests ($\chi^2$) and Cramer’s V in SPSS ($N = 65$ states, $n = 787$ differences) applying Resource Dependency & Policy Implementation theories.

Key Findings

Proved statistically significant dependencies between state status and compliance ($\chi^2(3) = 2.18, p < .001$), identifying Chapter 5 (Safety Data) as a primary SIDS bottleneck.

Strategic Recommendation

Designed the COMPASS Model to enable regional asset-pooling via Regional Safety Oversight Organizations (RSOOs) under ICAO's No Country Left Behind initiative.
